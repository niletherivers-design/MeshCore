# Experimental Methodology

MeshCore experiments are designed around reproducibility and explicit
separation between observation and authority.

Research practices include:

- preregistering important experimental criteria
- freezing implementations before measurement
- preserving negative and inconclusive outcomes
- comparing matched ACTION and CONTROL measurements
- checking semantic and decision parity
- separating diagnostic measurements from pass/fail gates
- preventing experimental shadow systems from silently changing production behavior

Public results are published only after the corresponding experiment has
completed and been reviewed.
