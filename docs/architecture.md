# MeshCore Architecture

MeshCore separates its experimental system into distinct authority domains.

## Vertical

Responsible for hierarchical physical storage organization and retrieval
planning.

## Horizontal

Responsible for predictive relationships between stored structures.

## Depth

Responsible for direct local experience and bounded action gating.

## Mirror

Mirror is an asynchronous experimental observation layer.

It may inspect contextual runtime information and classify optional actions
as beneficial, harmful, or uncertain, but the public research design does
not grant Mirror direct storage or production authority.
